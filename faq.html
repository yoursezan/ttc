Frequently Asked Questions

1. What technical courses are offered at TTC?

TTC offers industrial trades including Electrical Installation, Mechanical Lathe Machine Operation, Refrigeration & Air Conditioning, and IT Hardware Support.

2. How long are the training programs?

Program durations range from 3-month short certification modules to 6-month national level-3 vocational diplomas.

3. Are TTC certificates officially recognized?

Yes, all certifications issued by TTC are affiliated with the Bangladesh Technical Education Board (BTEB).

4. Is practical workshop training included?

Yes, 70% of the course duration consists of hands-on laboratory and workshop training under expert supervision.

5. How can I enroll in a course?

You can visit our campus directly or submit your details via the Contact Us page form. Our team will contact you.
